Getting Started

How do I create my first meeting?

To create a meeting:

  1. Log in to your dashboard
  2. Fill in the meeting details in the "Create Meeting" form
  3. Enter your Zoom Meeting ID and Password
  4. Specify the total number of members (must be divisible by 10)
  5. Set the number of video bots and audio bots
  6. Choose a name type (Indian or International)
  7. Select the meeting type
  8. Click "SUBMIT" to start the meeting

What are the requirements for total members?

The total number of members must be divisible by 10 and cannot be zero. For example, valid values are 10, 20, 30, 40, etc. This ensures proper bot allocation and meeting management.

How do I schedule a meeting for later?

To schedule a meeting:

  1. Fill in all meeting details as usual
  2. Check the "Enable Scheduling" checkbox
  3. Enter the scheduled time in IST (Indian Standard Time) format
  4. Click "SUBMIT"

The meeting will automatically start at the scheduled time.

Common Questions

What is the difference between "Normal Member" and "Profile Pic Member"?

Normal Member: Bots join with default Zoom avatars.

Profile Pic Member: Bots join with custom profile pictures from your uploaded collection.

How do I add custom names?

Click "Add custom names" in the header, select a name type (Indian or International), enter the name, and click "Add Name". These names will be available for use in future meetings.

What happens if I exceed my usage limit?

Your usage is tracked and displayed in the dashboard. If you approach or exceed your limit, you may need to upgrade your plan or wait for the next billing cycle. Contact support for assistance with usage limits.

Can I stop a meeting before it times out?

Yes, you can stop any active meeting by clicking the "Action" button in the "All Meetings" table. This will immediately end the meeting and disconnect all bots.

How do I download meeting details?

Click the "DOWNLOAD DETAIL'S" button in the "All Meetings" section. This will download a file containing all meeting information, including participant counts, timing, and configuration details.

Troubleshooting

Meeting fails to start

If your meeting fails to start, check:

  • Your Zoom Meeting ID and Password are correct
  • The meeting exists and is accessible
  • Your Zoom account has proper permissions
  • Your internet connection is stable
  • You haven't exceeded your usage limit

Bots are not joining the meeting

If bots aren't joining:

  • Verify the meeting ID and password are correct
  • Ensure the meeting allows participants to join
  • Check that your Zoom account settings allow bot participation
  • Review the meeting logs for error messages

I can't log in to my account

If you're having trouble logging in:

  • Verify your username and password are correct
  • Check that your account hasn't been suspended
  • Clear your browser cache and cookies
  • Try using a different browser
  • Contact support if the issue persists

Important: Always ensure you have valid Zoom credentials and that your Zoom account is in good standing. Issues with your Zoom account may affect our service.
